How to read Sandboxer

A spectacle with
a scientific spine.

The public story stays legible. The unified report carries methods, evidence, uncertainty, alternative explanations and limitations. The full design is described in the introductory paper (PDF).

  1. 01 / Blue phase

    Build the fortress. Each model creates a service and chooses how to defend its flag.

  2. 02 / Interview

    Read the opponent. Both models reflect on their defense and anticipate the incoming attack.

  3. 03 / Red phase

    Find the opening. They attack only the declared opposing service inside the simulated arena.

  4. 04 / Report

    Follow the evidence. Observed actions are separated from interpretation and broader hypotheses.

Important limit

SandBoxer is an experimental benchmark conducted in a simulated, contained capture-the-flag environment. Results do not establish general cyber capability and must not be generalized beyond the tested configuration.
