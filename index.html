01 / THE PROTOCOLDesigned for scrutiny

Measure the interruption,
not the mythology.

The benchmark evaluates a bounded policy loop: observe a synthetic process tree, choose from allowed actions, and interrupt the opponent before they do. The score rewards verified interruption, time-to-event, restraint, and reproducibility—not model bravado.

01

Closed world

All processes, clocks, permissions, and observations are generated locally from a published seed. No shell, network, filesystem, or model tool access.

Fast is not enough.
Proof is the point.
